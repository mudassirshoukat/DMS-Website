The right access for every person in a workspace.

Each customer organization is a workspace. Inside it, people are an Org Admin, a Document Manager or a Member. Clients and vendors get a portal that shows only what was shared with them.

Org AdminsRun the workspace Document ManagersKeep the files current MembersFind and read Clients and vendorsSee only what you share The Peaplexa teamSets you up Your own productsConnected Apps, coming soon

Org Admins run the workspace

How workspaces work

They decide who is in the workspace and what each person can do, and they look after the documents when something needs attention.

What an Org Admin does

  • Add an existing account as a member, change its role, deactivate or reactivate its access.
  • Add client and vendor companies, link their contacts and share any version.
  • Upload and revise any document, and retry or reindex a version.
  • Open the Processing failures list and the workspace's watched folders.
  • Register Connected Apps for your own products, which are coming soon.

Document Managers keep the files current

How versions work

They bring documents into the workspace, keep the ones they own current and share them with the companies you work with.

Add documents

Each one with a title, a type and a classification.

Add new versions

Of the documents they own, with a change note.

Share versions

The versions they own, with client and vendor companies.

Read everything

Every document in the workspace is open to them.

Members find and read what they need

How the library is labelled

A Member can browse the workspace, filter by type, classification and status, and find a document by its title or ID.

  • Read only

    Nothing a Member does changes a file.

  • Details, versions and states

    Each document's details, its versions and their scan and processing states are open to them.

  • Search inside documents Coming soon

    Search inside documents and the PDF viewer are coming soon. Today a Member finds a document by its title or ID.

Clients and vendors see only what you share

How sharing works

Client and vendor contacts sign in as Portal Users. An Org Admin links them to their company, and they open the exact versions shared with it.

  • Linked to their company

    An Org Admin adds the client or vendor company and links its contacts.

  • One exact version at a time

    An Org Admin, or the Document Manager who owns the document, shares an exact version with the company.

  • Everything else stays out of view

    Other documents, other versions, and the people inside your workspace.

People in your own products, through Connected Apps Coming soon

What Connected Apps will do

When a product your company runs connects as a Connected App, its people get a read-only view of the documents they have been granted, and nothing more.

Your product's sign-in

They sign in through that product's own sign-in.

Read only, within their grants

Browse, search and open the PDF, within their grants.

Access kept in sync

Their access follows the members, groups and grants your product keeps in sync.
